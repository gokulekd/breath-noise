Introduction

Welcome to Breath Noise, developed and operated by Gokul K S ("we," "our," or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and website.

By using Breath Noise, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy.

TL;DR: We collect minimal data, we never sell it, and we only use it to make the app better. You can request deletion of your data at any time.

2

Data We Collect

We collect only the minimum data necessary to provide a great experience:

  • Usage Analytics (Anonymous): Which features you use, session duration, and in-app interactions. This data is aggregated and never linked to your identity.
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system version, and app version to help us resolve crashes and improve compatibility.
  • Crash Reports: Anonymised crash logs and error traces sent automatically to help us fix bugs quickly.
  • Account Data (if signed in): If you choose to sign in with Google, we receive your name and email address solely to identify your account.
  • Preferences & Presets: Your sound mixer settings, timer preferences, and chosen scenes — stored locally on your device and optionally synced if you have an account.

We do not collect microphone audio, location data, contacts, photos, or any sensitive personal information.

3

How We Use Your Data

The data we collect is used exclusively for these purposes:

  • To provide, maintain, and improve the Breath Noise application and its features.
  • To understand how users interact with the app and identify areas for improvement.
  • To diagnose and fix technical issues, crashes, and errors.
  • To sync your preferences across devices (if you have an account).
  • To respond to your support requests or inquiries.

We will never sell your personal data, use it for advertising profiling, or share it with third parties for their own commercial purposes.

4

Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely using Google Firebase infrastructure, which complies with industry-standard security certifications including ISO 27001, SOC 1, SOC 2, and SOC 3.

Most of your preferences (sound mixes, timer settings) are stored locally on your device and are not transmitted unless you explicitly enable cloud sync. We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

While we strive to protect your data, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security but are committed to using best practices.

5

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services, each bound by their own privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase (Analytics & Crashlytics): For anonymous usage analytics and crash reporting. Firebase Privacy Policy ↗
  • Google Sign-In: For optional account authentication. Only your name and email are accessed. Google Privacy Policy ↗

These services access your information only to perform their designated tasks and are contractually prohibited from using it for other purposes.

6

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Request that we correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Deletion: Request that we delete all personal data associated with your account. Use our Data Deletion Request form.
  • Portability: Request that we provide your data in a portable format.
  • Objection: Object to certain types of processing of your data.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@breathnoise.life. We will respond within 30 days.

7

Children's Privacy

Breath Noise is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately at privacy@breathnoise.life and we will take steps to remove that information.

8

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, through an in-app notification.

We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically to stay informed about how we protect your information. Your continued use of Breath Noise after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
